Growth Lab

Assigned tests put unapproved drafts into arms (cut A/B/C, hook style), alternating by score, so you review exactly what will be posted. Observed tests compare a creative variable already recorded on each post (commentary, captions_burned, posting_hour_bucket ...). Frequency tests come from the Cadence page. Results use the evidence rule: ≥5 measured posts per arm before anything is said, ≥8 and q<0.05 for conclusive, per-1k-view rates only. Conclusive and suggestive results are written to the Knowledge Engine as PLN evidence.
