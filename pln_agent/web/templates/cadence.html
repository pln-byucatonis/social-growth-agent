Adaptive cadence

Each account starts from a configurable starting cadence (config/cadence.json — a test schedule, not a rule). Once ≥4 weeks are measured, the recommendation follows incremental followers per extra post, engagement dilution in busier weeks and the drafts we can actually supply. The calendar planner caps each account at its recommended weekly number.
