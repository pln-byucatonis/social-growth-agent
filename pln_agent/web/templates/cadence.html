Cadence & posting times

How often each account should post and when. Every account starts on a starting cadence (a test, not a rule). After 4+ measured weeks the recommendation follows the extra followers each additional post brings, whether busier weeks dilute engagement, and how many drafts we can actually supply. Posting times are learned per account; one slot a day stays exploratory.
